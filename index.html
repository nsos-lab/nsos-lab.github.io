Research

Four directions, one mission.

Our work spans the full stack of modern networked systems — from kernel-level observability primitives to the safety of the autonomous agents running on top.

🔭

System Observability

eBPF-based full-stack instrumentation to continuously monitor networked systems with minimal overhead, enabling kernel-to-application tracing in production.

eBPF Tracing Low Overhead
🤖

Agentic AI Safety

Runtime security, resource abuse detection, and dependability analysis for LLM-based autonomous agents — bridging OS-level enforcement and agent reasoning.

LLM Agents Runtime Safety Dependability
🛡️

Network Security

Intrusion detection, anomaly analysis, and traffic forensics for modern distributed systems, with an emphasis on encrypted traffic and protocol-level reasoning.

IDS Traffic Analysis Forensics
🔍

Vulnerability Research

Automated detection and responsible disclosure of system-level vulnerabilities, including fuzzing, symbolic execution, and zero-day discovery in agents and networks.

Fuzzing Zero-day Responsible Disclosure
